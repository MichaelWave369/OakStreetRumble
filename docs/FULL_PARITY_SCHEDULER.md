# Full parity scheduler and derived actions

This rung restores the scheduling detail that matters for deterministic agent
control: bodies are observed **when they are about to act**, not all at once at
the beginning of the tick.

```text
external/session roots
        |
        v
human controller observations
        |
        v
simulation tick
  player  -> observe local lead if authoritative -> act
  partner -> observe partner at the now-current world -> act
  Wren    -> observe after player + partner -> act
  rival   -> observe after the crew -> act
        |
        v
enemy/world rules
        |
        v
ledger + replay frame
```

That preserves the verified leader -> partner -> Wren -> rival ordering from the
Sol handoff while keeping gameplay behind the `OakRules` seam.

## Derived room transitions

Crossing an exit no longer lets the gameplay adapter mutate rooms as an invisible
side effect.

A validated movement action can request traversal:

```text
MOVE a:41
   |
   +-> engine reaches exit
         |
         +-> derived ENTER_ROOM a:42
               causalParent = a:41
               controller = controller that actually moved
               |
               +-> navigation authority check
               +-> OakRules validation
               +-> room mutation
               +-> ledger receipt
```

If movement and navigation authority are split between controllers, the mover
cannot impersonate the navigation controller. The derived transition is rejected
and retains its causal parent.

Automatic traversal is generated by `bot:lead` through the same `ENTER_ROOM`
action grammar and navigation authority.

## Replay rule

Only external/resolved roots are stored in replay frames. Deterministic local bot
decisions and derived actions are regenerated at their exact within-tick point.
This keeps replay compact while making scheduling part of the deterministic
runtime contract.
